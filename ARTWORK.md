# Портреты клиентов — версия 9

Созданы встроенным инструментом ImageGen. Каждый PNG — отдельный персонаж и четыре его эмоции в сетке 2 × 2. Верхний ряд: уверенность, тревога; нижний: облегчение, потрясение. Исходные запросы ниже.

Restrained desaturated palette, black grey ivory muted slate-blue and subdued character-specific colors, no bright neon. Transparent background, no text, letters, watermark, UI, grid lines, scenery or labels. Character must remain recognizable at 110px. Keep each emotional variant equally detailed. Generate one single image. Character design: An extravagant upscale restaurant toilet, distinctive tall elegant elongated Art Deco silhouette, expensive dark navy-black marble cistern with pale veining, ivory narrow bowl and gold/brass rim, four little ornate gold feet, gold monocle on one eye, thin curled moustache, black bow tie, haughty raised chin posture. The whole object is bespoke luxurious furniture, not a generic white toilet with a hat.
